Methodology

  1. Intelligence I: the arithmetic mean of Terminal-Bench 4.0 (agentic terminal coding), Humanity’s Last Exam (multidisciplinary reasoning) and AutomationBench (SaaS workflow automation), all independently measured by Artificial Analysis under a unified harness — not vendor-reported.
  2. Speed S and token use N: both AA-measured. S is output speed in tokens/s; N is the average output tokens (including reasoning) per benchmark task, so verbose models are charged for their real cost.
  3. Intelligence density D = I/100 × S: quality-weighted tokens per second.
  4. Intelligence per unit V = I ÷ (N/1M × output price): the benchmark points one yuan (or dollar) buys on a typical task. Speed is not part of V; it is captured by D.
  5. Dual currency: models with official dual pricing (DeepSeek, Kimi, Qwen, GLM, Mimo) are computed from each official price independently; USD-only models convert at 1 USD = 7.2 CNY.
  6. Sorting: default is intelligence per unit in this page’s currency (descending); click any column header to re-sort.

FAQ

How are the intelligence index and intelligence per unit computed?
The intelligence index is the average of three independently measured benchmarks: Terminal-Bench 4.0, Humanity’s Last Exam and AutomationBench. Intelligence per unit = intelligence index ÷ cost per task, where cost per task = measured output tokens per task × the model’s output price — so a verbose model can offer poor value despite a low unit price.
Where does the data come from?
Capability, speed and token use are independently measured by Artificial Analysis (unified harness, not vendor-reported); prices come from official provider pricing pages. For example, Claude Sonnet 5.5 measures 55.0% on HLE, matching Anthropic’s published 54.9%.
Why isn’t the fastest model the best value?
Intelligence per unit measures how much intelligence one unit of money buys; speed is captured separately by intelligence density (D = I/100 × speed). Gemini 3.8 Flash tops density, but its higher token use puts it mid-table on value. Watch the density column for throughput and the value columns for cost.
